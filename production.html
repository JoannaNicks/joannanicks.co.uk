Podcasts, video and social content

Production, editing and editorial support for organisations, businesses and creative projects.

I help turn ideas, conversations and footage into polished content, including podcasts, video and social media content. With a background in writing and production, I bring an editorial eye to every project, from shaping an idea and finding the focus to editing the final piece.

Services

Podcast Production

From pre-production through to the finished episode.

I can help with planning, editing, show notes, hosting account set-up and publishing, as well as creating content to support a podcast launch and ongoing episode releases. I can also advise on marketing strategy, including SEO and keywords.

Video Editing

Interviews, events, promotional content, documentaries and everything in between.

I take the footage you have and shape it into a high-quality, engaging finished piece, with clear structure, strong pacing and an eye for what keeps people watching. I can also add captions, graphics and create short-form cutdowns for social media.

Social Content

Graphics, captions and short-form video created for social platforms, including clips and cutdowns from longer-form content.

I can help with everything from ideas and copy to graphics, short-form video and scheduling. I keep platform best practice, formats and current trends in mind, so the content feels relevant, engaging and suited to each platform.

Membership Content

Written and video content, email newsletters and full membership sites.

I can build and curate membership content across formats, from video and written pieces to email newsletters. I’ve created video content for Patreon, and built an entire membership site on Kajabi for a coaching client.

Creative Content Support

Not every project fits neatly into a box.

I work across video, audio, copywriting and social content, so I can step in wherever you need me, shaping ideas, problem solving, creating assets or simply taking care of whatever your project needs next.
