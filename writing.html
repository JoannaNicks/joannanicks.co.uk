Writing

Work for stage and screen

I’m an award-winning writer based in West Yorkshire, writing for stage and screen.

My play And It Rains won the Shelagh Delaney New Writing Award. My work has been longlisted for the Bruntwood Prize, the Theatre503 International Playwriting Award and the Sky Studios & Box of Tricks Screen/Play Award, and placed in the top 12% of the BBC Writersroom Open Call.

I’m drawn to character-led stories, sharp dialogue and work that finds humour in complicated, sometimes uncomfortable places.
